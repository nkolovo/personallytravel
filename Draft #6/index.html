The Cyclades, slowly
10 days · Milos, Folegandros, Santorini · for Helen & Marcus
EKPlanned by Eleni

Arrive, exhale, swim

Athens → Milos
  1. 07:15
    Fast ferry from Piraeus
    Seats reserved on the upper deck, port side — for the first glimpse of the islands.
  2. 10:40
    Nikos meets you at Adamas port
    Bags to your suite in Pollonia; you go straight to the water.
  3. 13:30
    Lunch at a family taverna by the harbour
    Order whatever the fishermen brought in that morning.
  4. 19:20
    Sunset at Sarakiniko
    The moon-white rocks turn pink. Arrive 40 minutes early for the best ledge.

Bring a light layer for Sarakiniko — the rocks hold the wind after dark.

— Eleni

Sea caves & salt hair

Around Milos by boat
  1. 09:30
    Private caïque to Kleftiko
    Before the day boats arrive — you'll have the arches almost to yourselves.
  2. 12:00
    Swim stop at Gerakas
    Reachable only by sea. Snorkels on board.
  3. 13:30
    Lunch on deck
    Grilled octopus and island wine, cooked by Captain Stavros.
  4. 20:30
    Evening wander in Plaka
    No plans. Follow the lanes up to the castle for the lights.
Plan BIf the meltemi picks up, the boat switches to the sheltered south coast — already arranged with the captain.

Ask Stavros about the pirates of Kleftiko. He tells it better than any guidebook.

— Eleni

The island time forgot

Milos → Folegandros
  1. 11:10
    Short hop to Folegandros
    Fewer than 800 people live here year-round. You'll see why they stay.
  2. 13:00
    Check in above the cliffs
    A cave-style suite with its own terrace over the Aegean.
  3. 18:45
    Walk up to Panagia church
    A zig-zag path, 20 minutes, the whole island glowing below you.
  4. 20:30
    Dinner in Chora's squares
    Try matsata — the island's hand-rolled pasta.

The table by the stone wall is the one. I've asked for it.

— Eleni

Santorini, the quiet way

Folegandros → Santorini
  1. 10:00
    Ferry to Santorini, driver waiting
    Straight past the port queues to your hotel in Imerovigli.
  2. 15:00
    Volcanic wine tasting
    A small family estate — vines woven into baskets against the wind.
  3. 19:40
    Sunset dinner, Imerovigli
    Same caldera view as Oia, a fraction of the crowd.
Plan BFerry strike? A private speedboat is on standby — you won't lose the evening.

Walk to Skaros Rock at dawn tomorrow. You'll thank me.

— Eleni
